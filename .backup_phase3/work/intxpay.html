IntXPay

A fintech platform for businesses worldwide — global payments, crypto trading and financial solutions, all in one place.

01 · Overview

IntXPay is a fintech platform for businesses operating worldwide: international transfers, multi-currency accounts, SWIFT and SEPA, crypto exchange and AML/KYC compliance.

Cross-border payments feel complex and risky, so the brand had to signal speed and trust at a glance. I set the strategy and a precise, jargon-free voice, then drew an X of four symmetrical arrows in yellow, black and white with an orange accent, set in Switzer, with custom icons and a 4×4 social grid.

A 46-page brand guideline covering stationery, merch, posters, stickers, banners and the website — every application follows the same rules, so IntXPay is recognisable at a glance.

02 · Challenge & approach

Cross-border payments and crypto feel complex and risky. The brand had to signal speed and trust at a glance and speak to businesses clearly and confidently — without financial jargon.

  1. 01

    Strategy

    Mission, vision and a voice that is professional yet human — precise and jargon-free.

  2. 02

    Identity

    An X of four symmetrical arrows; yellow, black and white with an orange accent; Switzer type.

  3. 03

    System

    Custom icons with rounded corners and cut edges, social templates on a 4×4 grid and a photography guide.

  4. 04

    Guideline

    A 46-page brand guideline: stationery, merch, posters, stickers, banners and the website in practice.
