SkyBiz

Online business services for small businesses, freelancers and startups in the USA — making numbers feel light.

01 · Overview

SkyBiz is a US online business-services company: bookkeeping and tax filing, business registration, financial advice and notary services for small business owners, freelancers and startups.

Accounting and compliance weigh small businesses down, so the brand had to make numbers feel light and talk like a person, not a bank. I built it end to end — mission and voice, a paper-plane logo, a sky palette, Nunito type and icons — then designed the website from one UI kit, with motion and an illustration for every service.

A full brandbook — logo rules, colours, type, icons, social media and stationery — and a light, sky-toned website: main page, About us, four service pages, pricing, blog and contact, where every service page tells the same story so clients find help fast.

02 · Challenge & approach

Accounting and compliance weigh small businesses down. The brand had to make numbers feel light and turn technical language into human conversations — without corporate stiffness.
