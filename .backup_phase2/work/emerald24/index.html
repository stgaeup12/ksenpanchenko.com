Emerald24

A companion bank website for business and private clients — solid as a stone, smart as a gem.

01 · Overview

Emerald24 is a UK e-money institution: multi-currency IBAN accounts, cards, acquiring and white-label payments.

Clients are tired of banks that act like a policeman, so the site had to feel friendly but serious. I audited 16 competitor sites, from Revolut and Wise to HSBC, wrote two personas, mapped the structure and flows, wireframed 13 page types and built a green glass visual language with a responsive UI.

A UI kit and production-ready designs: one long homepage read like a film strip and four product pages with the same rhythm. The website won two Gold awards at the London Design Awards.

02 · Challenge & approach

Clients are tired of banks that act like a policeman — endless forms, unexplained blocks. The site had to feel friendly but serious and earn trust without walls of text.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Audit of 16 competitor sites, from Revolut and Wise to HSBC, plus two personas and the brand story.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Website map, page structure and user flows; wireframes for 13 page types, from Home to 404.

  3. 03

    Design

    Moodboard and colour directions, a green glass visual language and responsive UI for every page.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    UI kit — buttons, inputs, menus, header and footer — and production-ready page designs.
