I’m Ksen — a UX/UI & Product Designer. I design websites, products and design systems that make complex things feel obvious.

I work independently with FinTech, SaaS and web teams. If it needs explaining, it needs redesigning — so I start with research, build systems rather than screens, and stay with developers until the final QA.

Why glyphs

Every interface starts as characters — letters, numbers, a blinking cursor. So every drawing here is live text: the thorn garland, my portrait, the 404 zero. Each one assembles from random symbols, the way a product comes together from research, notes and rough flows — until it clicks.

FinTech and SaaS are full of noise: fees, limits, compliance, five steps where two would do. My job is to turn it into something you understand at a glance.

Try it
