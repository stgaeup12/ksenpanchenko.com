Trulia

A UX/UI concept for the Trulia real estate app — discover a home and a neighbourhood you’ll love to live in.

01 · Overview

Trulia helps people buy or rent a home with neighbourhood photos, resident reviews and local insights. This is a concept redesign of its mobile app.

Finding a home means two lists and a lot of patience, so the search had to feel calm and local. I started with competitor analysis, user interviews, personas and a journey map, then built the app map and wireframes and tested a functional and an interactive prototype with users.

140+ screens for iOS with Android and web adaptations, a design system and a new app icon — with search at the centre and every listing opening into its neighbourhood.

02 · Challenge & approach

Finding a home means two lists, dozens of papers and a lot of patience. The app had to make the search feel calm and local and keep every step in one place — without endless listings.

  1. 01

    Research

    Competitor analysis, user interviews, an empathy map, personas and a customer journey map.

  2. 02

    Structure

    A mind map, the app map and wireframes for search, listings, map view and booking.

  3. 03

    Prototype

    A functional, then an interactive prototype with ease-out 300 ms transitions, tested with users.

  4. 04

    Design

    A 60+ image moodboard, a design system and 140+ screens for iOS, Android and web, plus the app icon.

04 · The screens

Search sits at the centre: filters, saved searches and the map are one tap away, and every listing opens into the neighbourhood — schools, crime, documents and similar homes — before you book a viewing.
